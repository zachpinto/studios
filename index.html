How this map is built

The opening view sizes each company by its share of 2025 US and Canada ticket sales (Comscore figures reported by CNBC, with Screendollars figures for the smaller distributors). Skydance combines Warner Bros. and Paramount; that merger is scheduled to close on October 6, 2026.

Inside each company, circles are sized by the combined worldwide gross of the titles listed. Grosses are unadjusted for inflation, rounded, and compiled by hand from Box Office Mojo and The Numbers figures, so treat them as approximate. 2026 releases reflect totals as of mid-2026.

TV and streaming brands rank their shows by the number of IMDb user ratings, a rough popularity measure; those counts are approximate. A show is filed under the network or streamer where it premiered when that network is on this map, and otherwise under the studio that made it, with the other side noted. Where a company holds both film and TV, its two halves are sized by how many brands each contains.

Only brands each company controls are shown. Joint ventures such as A+E Networks are left out, as are the cable networks Comcast spun off into Versant in January 2026 and Starz, which split from Lionsgate in 2025.

Each film sits under the label most tied to it, with co-producers and outside distributors noted beside it. Lists cover each brand's biggest titles rather than complete filmographies. Logos identify each company and belong to their respective owners. Posters are not reproduced: each title gets a generated film frame instead. Titles link to Wikipedia.
